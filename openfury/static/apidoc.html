Screening API

Send a candidate, a job and a use case. Our voice agent phones the candidate, runs a structured screen, and posts back a verdict, the captured fields, a timestamped transcript, the full call log and a recording link. You can follow the transcript live while the call runs. The agent introduces itself under your recruiter name and your company. The candidate never hears ours.

Base URL{{PUBLIC_BASE_URL}}/v1
FormatJSON in, JSON out. Timestamps are ISO 8601 UTC. Phone numbers are E.164.
AuthAuthorization: Bearer of_live_… or of_test_…
DeliverySigned webhooks to the URL on your account, plus GET as a fallback.

What the job-screening agent does on every call (the language-freelancer agent is described under Use cases):

  1. It opens with: “Hi Ravi, this is Priya from Acme Talent. I'm calling about a Backend Engineer role at Acme. Do you have two minutes?”
  2. If the candidate is busy, it books a callback time. If they're not interested, it accepts the first no. If it's the wrong person, it apologises. If they ask to be removed, it confirms and flags it.
  3. It covers the deal-breakers first: notice period, current and expected CTC, location and work mode, competing offers, experience. A firm mismatch ends the call politely.
  4. Only then does it pitch the role, ask the role-specific questions it writes from your job brief, and ask your custom questions.
  5. After the call, a separate review pass reads the transcript and produces the result. The verdict rules are mechanical, so an optimistic “Proceed” never reaches you on a candidate who is over budget or over notice.

Quickstart

1. Place a call with your test key. Nobody is dialled. You get the whole lifecycle and every webhook within about 10 seconds.

curl {{PUBLIC_BASE_URL}}/v1/calls \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $OF_TEST_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: cand-881-backend-attempt-1" \
  -d '{
    "candidate": { "name": "Ravi Kumar", "phone": "+919876543210" },
    "job": {
      "role_name": "Backend Engineer",
      "client_company": "Acme",
      "role_location": "Bangalore",
      "work_mode": "hybrid",
      "experience_range": "3 to 6 years",
      "ctc_min": "18 lakhs per annum",
      "ctc_max": "30 lakhs per annum",
      "max_notice_period": "60 days",
      "role_description": "Python, Kafka, Postgres; owns the payments ledger service."
    },
    "agent": { "name": "Priya", "company_name": "Acme Talent" },
    "custom_fields": [
      { "key": "open_to_night_shift", "question": "Are you open to a night shift once a month?" }
    ],
    "external_ref": "cand-881",
    "metadata": { "crm_candidate_id": 881, "requisition": "REQ-204" }
  }'

2. Receive the webhooks. You'll get call.status four times (dialing, ringing, in_progress, processing), then one call.completed carrying everything. Verify the signature, store the payload, and answer 2xx.

3. Switch to your live key. Same request, real call. Download the recording before retention.delete_after.

Check your webhook endpoint at any time with POST /v1/webhooks/test. It sends a signed webhook.test event and tells you what your server answered.

Authentication & test mode

Every account has two keys. They differ only in what they do:

KeyEffect
of_live_…Real phone calls, inside calling hours, within your concurrency limit.
of_test_…No call is placed. The full status sequence runs in about 10 seconds, and call.completed carries a realistic sample result built from your job brief, with "test": true. Calling hours don't apply.

Calls made with one key can't be seen with the other. Keys are secrets for your server only. Never put them in a browser or mobile app. If a key leaks, rotate it (python -m openfury.api tenant-set <id> --rotate-keys). The old key stops working immediately.

Your data, not ours

We are a pipe, not a database. Everything a call produces is delivered to you, and you are the system of record.

  • After the call.completed webhook is delivered and the retention window has passed (24 hours by default, up to 72 on request), we delete the transcript, result, call log, recording, the request you sent and the phone number.
  • Until then, GET /v1/calls/{id} returns the full payload, and the recording link works. After deletion, GET returns only ids, timestamps, outcome and duration ("purged": true), and the recording link returns 410.
  • If your endpoint is down, we keep retrying for about 16 hours and hold the data until delivery succeeds or retries are exhausted. After that the retention window applies.
  • One exception: when a candidate asks on the call not to be contacted again, we keep a salted hash of their number on your account, so that no future call ever reaches them. See Suppressions.
Download the recording. recording.url is a time-limited link. Fetch the MP3 into your own storage when you receive call.completed. Don't store our link.

Verdict rules, in order: voicemail if no human answered. Then callback_scheduled, not_interested or wrong_person if the call ended that way. Then not_qualified if the candidate firmly has no laptop, speaks and writes none of the languages, or can't hold a basic English conversation. Then qualified if they have a laptop, can communicate in English, and speak and write at least one of the languages. Otherwise incomplete.

Call lifecycle

queued→dialing→ringing→in_progress→processing→ended

A call that is never answered goes from ringing straight to ended. If you asked for retries, it goes back to queued instead and keeps the same call id. attempt counts the dials. processing is the 10–60 seconds after hang-up while the result and recording are produced.

Outcomes

Every ended call has exactly one outcome:

outcomeMeaningHas result?
answeredA person picked up and the call ran. See end_reason and result.verdict for how it went.Yes (null if nobody spoke)
voicemailAn answering machine or IVR picked up. The agent hung up without leaving a message.Usually no
no_answerRang for 45 s unanswered, or was rejected or unreachable, and no retries were left.No
failedThe call could not be placed. error says why.No
cancelledYou cancelled it.Only if it had already been answered
suppressedThe number was added to your do-not-call list after this call was queued, so it was never dialled.No

end_reason on answered calls

completed · not_interested · callback · wrong_person · removed · time_cap · voicemail · no_response · candidate_hung_up

The agent ends the call itself. After its closing line it hangs up within a few seconds. If the candidate goes silent for 15 seconds, it checks once whether they're still there. After a second silence it says goodbye and hangs up, with end_reason: no_response.

Real-time transcript

You can follow a call while it happens, for example to show a recruiter the conversation live in your CRM. Each finished turn (one person's sentence or two, once they stop talking) is available about 1–2 seconds after it's spoken. There are three ways to get it, and you can use any of them:

WayBest forHow
Webhook per turnSystems that already take our webhooksSet "realtime_transcript": true on POST /v1/calls. Each turn arrives as a call.transcript event at your webhook URL.
Event streamA live view in your appGET /v1/calls/{id}/stream streams server-sent events: status, transcript, then one completed, after which the connection closes. Works for any call and needs no flag.
PollingSimple scriptsGET /v1/calls/{id} while status is in_progress or processing includes live_transcript: every finished turn so far.

A turn looks the same in all three:

{ "speaker": "candidate", "text": "Sixty days, but buyout is possible.", "at_s": 41.3, "interrupted": false }
// speaker: "agent" | "candidate"   at_s: seconds since the agent joined the call
// interrupted: true when the agent was cut off mid-sentence by the candidate

The call.transcript webhook carries data: {id, external_ref, metadata, index, turn}. index starts at 0 and has no gaps. Webhooks can arrive out of order, so sort by index. The authoritative transcript, with per-turn latency and speech-recognition confidence, is the one in call.completed.

Only finished turns are sent, never half-recognised words, so text you've already shown never changes. Your API key must stay on your server, so to show a live transcript in a browser, open the stream from your backend and relay it to the page.

Calling rules

These rules are enforced for every account. They protect the candidates and your sender reputation.

RuleDetail
NumbersMobile numbers in E.164 (+<country><number>). A bare 10-digit number is read as Indian (DEFAULT_COUNTRY_CODE).
Calling hours09:30–20:30 in the account's timezone by default (adjustable per account; default Asia/Kolkata). Calls queued outside the window wait for it. They are not dropped.
ConcurrencyUp to 10 simultaneous calls per account. Send as many calls as you like: anything over 10 waits in the queue in schedule_at order and starts as soon as a slot frees up, with no error and nothing to retry on your side. Queued calls stay queued until they dial. GET /v1/account shows your limit and how many calls are open in each status. The operator can raise it per account (--concurrency).
One open call per numberA second call to a number that already has a queued or live call is refused with 409 call_in_progress.
Do-not-callA number on your suppression list is refused with 409 do_not_call. Candidates who ask to be removed on a call are added automatically.
Ringing45 seconds, then no_answer.
Lengthjob-screening: the agent starts wrapping up at 10 minutes and the call ends at 12. language-freelancer: wraps up at 5 minutes and ends at 7.
AI disclosureIf a candidate asks whether they're speaking to an AI, the agent says yes. It always does, and this can't be configured.
Fixed behaviourThe voice rules, safety rules (never asks for bank or payment details, never states a salary or offer that is not in your brief, never asks about religion, caste, marital status, health, age or other protected topics) and verdict rules are fixed. You shape what is asked. You can't change how the agent behaves.

A : keep-alive comment is sent about every second. After completed (whose data is the full call object) the server closes the connection. GET

/v1/calls/{id}

The call as it stands. Once status is ended, this returns the same body as the call.completed webhook (with a fresh recording link) until the data is deleted. Use it to reconcile if you missed a webhook. Don't poll it in a tight loop. Webhooks are the primary channel.

GET

/v1/calls

Summaries, newest first. Query: status, external_ref, limit (1–200, default 50), before (a call id, for paging). The response is {"data": [...], "has_more", "next_before"}. Summaries don't include results. Fetch a call by id for those.

POST

/v1/calls/{id}/cancel

A queued call ends at once with outcome: cancelled. A ringing call is hung up. A call in progress is hung up mid-conversation. That's abrupt for the candidate, so use it only when you must. If the call was already answered, the result is still produced and delivered. Returns 409 already_ended if it has already ended.

POST

/v1/calls/{id}/redeliver

Queue the call.completed webhook again, for example after fixing a bug in your handler. Only works before the data is deleted.

GET

/v1/calls/{id}/recording?exp=…&sig=…

The stereo MP3, 96 kbps: left channel = candidate, right channel = agent, on one aligned timeline. The link is self-authenticating, so don't send your API key with it. Get it from recording.url. It returns 410 after recording.expires_at.

POST

/v1/suppressions

Add a number to your account's do-not-call list. Body: {"phone": "+919876543210", "reason": "optional"}. Queued calls to that number end as suppressed and are never dialled. We store only a salted hash.

DELETE

/v1/suppressions/{phone}

Remove a number, for example when a candidate opts back in. Returns {"removed": true|false}.

GET

Your settings (webhook URL, concurrency, calling hours, retention, agent defaults) and a live count of open calls by status, for the key's mode.

Verifying signatures

Compute HMAC-SHA256 with your whsec_… secret over the string "{t}.{raw body}", compare it in constant time with v1, and reject a t more than 5 minutes old. Use the raw request body. Re-serialised JSON won't match.

import hashlib, hmac, time

def verify(raw_body: bytes, header: str, secret: str) -> bool:
    parts = dict(p.split("=", 1) for p in header.split(","))
    t, sig = parts["t"], parts["v1"]
    if abs(time.time() - int(t)) > 300:
        return False
    want = hmac.new(secret.encode(), f"{t}.".encode() + raw_body, hashlib.sha256).hexdigest()
    return hmac.compare_digest(want, sig)

# Flask:   verify(request.get_data(), request.headers["X-Webhook-Signature"], WHSEC)
# FastAPI: verify(await request.body(), request.headers["x-webhook-signature"], WHSEC)
const crypto = require("crypto");

function verify(rawBody, header, secret) {
  const parts = Object.fromEntries(header.split(",").map(p => p.split("=")));
  if (Math.abs(Date.now() / 1000 - Number(parts.t)) > 300) return false;
  const want = crypto.createHmac("sha256", secret)
    .update(`${parts.t}.${rawBody}`).digest("hex");
  return crypto.timingSafeEqual(Buffer.from(want), Buffer.from(parts.v1));
}

Retries & ordering

  • Any 2xx within 15 seconds counts as delivered. Anything else, including a timeout, is retried after 30 s, 2 min, 10 min, 30 min, 1 h, 2 h, 4 h and 8 h. After that the delivery is marked failed and shows in GET /v1/deliveries.
  • Answer fast and do the work afterwards: store the body, return 200, then process it.
  • Delivery is at least once. De-duplicate on the envelope id.
  • Events can arrive out of order when a retry is involved. Use data.sequence on call.status to discard stale ones, and treat call.completed as final whenever it arrives.

The result

This section describes job-screening. For language-freelancer, see its result fields. Both share verdict, summary, fields, structured, risk_flags, custom and do_not_call.

verdict

The verdict is applied in this order, mechanically, so you can act on it without listening to the call:

verdictRule
callback_scheduled / not_interestedThe call ended that way. For a callback, the time is in fields.callback_time.
rejectA firm mismatch: irrelevant experience; expected CTC above budget and the candidate didn't accept the budget; notice above the maximum and not negotiable; location or work mode refused.
holdA soft mismatch (“negotiable”, buyout possible), any must-capture item not captured, or a competing offer.
proceedNone of the above: relevant, within budget or accepted it, notice within the maximum, location and mode accepted, and everything captured.

fields

All values are strings, and anything not covered is exactly "Not captured". Keys: candidate_name, current_company_and_role, total_experience, key_responsibilities, reason_for_leaving, desired_next_role, current_ctc, expected_ctc, expected_ctc_basis, budget_disclosed_and_reaction, notice_period, notice_negotiable_or_buyout, last_working_day, gap_reason, current_location, open_to_location_and_mode, relocation_timeline, callback_time, not_interested_reason, asked_to_be_removed, open_questions, other_offers.

structured

Numbers for writing straight into your ATS: CTC in lakhs per annum and notice in days. A value is null if it wasn't said. It is never guessed from a job title.

risk_flags

Zero or more of: expected_above_budget, competing_offer, notice_above_max, location_or_mode_mismatch, skills_depth_unclear.

do_not_call

If this is true, the candidate asked to be removed. We have already suppressed the number on your account. Mark them in your own system too, and stop all other outreach to them.

Errors

Every error on /v1 has the same shape:

{ "error": { "code": "invalid_request", "message": "job: job is missing required fields: ctc_max" } }
HTTPcodeMeaning
401unauthorizedMissing, malformed or revoked key.
404not_foundNo such call for this key, including calls made with your other key.
409call_in_progressThis number already has an open call.
409do_not_callThe number is on your suppression list.
409already_ended, not_available, no_webhookThe action doesn't fit the call's (or the account's) current state.
410expired, deletedThe recording is past its retention window.
422invalid_request, invalid_use_case, invalid_phone, invalid_voiceThe body failed validation. The message lists every problem.
5xx—Our fault. Retry with the same Idempotency-Key. It's safe.

Your responsibilities

  • Consent and lawful basis. Call only candidates who applied, or who consented to be contacted about roles, and keep that evidence in your system. You are the data fiduciary for your candidates under the DPDP Act, 2023. We process their data on your instructions and delete it as described in Your data, not ours.
  • Recording notice. Calls are recorded. Make sure your candidate-facing privacy notice says that screening calls may be recorded and handled by an AI agent.
  • Do-not-call. Respect do_not_call: true across all of your outreach, not just these calls. Push your own opt-outs to us with POST /v1/suppressions.
  • Accuracy of the brief. The agent says what the brief says. A wrong budget in the brief becomes a wrong budget on the phone.
  • Decisions. The verdict is a screening recommendation. A person on your side makes the hiring decision.
